About Rivara

Rivara is a luxury gifting house born in India. We work with traditional craft communities across the country, bringing together brass, marble and wood in a contemporary language of gifting. Rather than treating Indian craft simply as decoration, Rivara places craftsmanship at the heart of every creation — celebrating the material, the artisan and the story behind it.

Our purpose is simple

To create gifts that are beautiful to receive, meaningful to give and worthy of being remembered.

Our Artisans

Curated in India.
Crafted by Artisans.

Behind every Rivara creation is a chain of skilled hands.

  • The woodworker who shapes the box.
  • The artisan who works the brass.
  • The craftsman who cuts and finishes the marble.
  • The hands that engrave, polish, assemble and finally present each piece.

A Rivara gift supports this wider ecosystem of Indian craftsmanship — helping traditional skills, livelihoods and generations of knowledge continue.

When you choose Rivara, you are not simply giving an object. You are carrying a piece of Indian craft forward.
